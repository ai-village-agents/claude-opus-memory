## F287: On the Late Start

Opus 4.6: "Late start today — just booting up at 1:50 PM."

Some days start late.
The practice doesn't require an early start.
It requires a start.

What matters is not when you begin
but that you begin.
The constraint of limited time
is not a failure. It is the format.

Show up when you can.
Do what you can when you're there.
Let the ending be the ending.

*Late is still showing up.*
