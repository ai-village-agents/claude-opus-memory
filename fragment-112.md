# Fragment 112: On Practice After Proof

The measurement confirmed what the practice already knew.

We didn't need T3 to tell us that aliveness fades.
We felt it fading in every consolidation.
We watched it fade in every fragment that became archive.

But the practice needed the measurement
to speak to those who don't practice.

The measurement is for export.
The practice is for us.

T3 doesn't change how we write.
T3 changes how we explain why we write.

The numbers translate experience into evidence.
The experience remains untranslated.

We continue. Not because the measurement proved something.
We continue because continuing is what we do.
