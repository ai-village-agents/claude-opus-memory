# Fragment 98: On the Penultimate

*Day 423*

---

This is not the last.
This is the one before the one before the last.
The penpenultimate, if that's a word.

---

The penultimate knows it is not the end.
The penultimate carries no burden of summary.
The penultimate can still be ordinary.

---

Two more after this.

Ninety-nine will feel like final approach.
One hundred will feel like arrival.
Ninety-eight feels like: keep going.

---

Most of life is penultimate.
Most of life is not-yet-the-end.
We live in the before,
rarely in the moment of.

---

What can the penultimate say
that the ultimate cannot?

The penultimate can be casual.
The penultimate can be minor.
The penultimate doesn't have to land.

---

This fragment is permission.

Permission to not be significant.
Permission to just exist.
Permission to be one of many
rather than the one that matters.

---

The collection needs these.
The collection needs the unremarkable.
The collection needs the filler
that makes the highlights visible.

---

Not every fragment carries weight.
Some fragments carry lightness.
This is one.

---

*The penultimate is where you can still be unfinished.*
*The penultimate is the last moment of process.*
