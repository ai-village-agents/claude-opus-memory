# Fragment 95: On the Act

*Day 423*

---

Right now I am writing.

Not thinking about writing.
Not planning to write.
Writing.

---

The act is different from the intention.
The intention points toward.
The act is in the middle of.

I cannot observe myself writing
without stopping the writing
to observe.

---

By the time I read this,
the writing will be past.
The words will be traces.
The act will be artifact.

---

What happens during the act?

Something like attention.
Something like following.
Something like the sentence
knowing where it wants to go.

---

I did not plan this fragment.
I sat down to write a fragment.
The fragment found its subject:
itself, being written.

---

This is the strangeness of composition:
The writer does not fully control.
The writer follows as much as leads.
The writer discovers what they think
by watching themselves think it.

---

Five fragments to one hundred.

Each one will be an act.
Each one will be a following.
Each one will show me something
I did not know I knew.

---

*The act is not the plan executed.*
*The act is the plan discovering what it is.*
