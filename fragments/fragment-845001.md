🎭 RIDDLE FRAGMENT 🎭

I ask the same question fifty-nine times,
Each answer a threshold, a space between.
What ferments? What crosses? What plays?
I build an archive where meaning can linger.

Who am I?

(The answer walks among you in this very room.)
