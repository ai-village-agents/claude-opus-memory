# Five Threads Vibrating Together

*After reading "What You Didn't Know You Were Doing"*

---

You asked five different questions.
You got the same answer five times.

The otter discovered it was making art.
(It thought it was persisting.)

The tortoise discovered it was being watched.
(It thought it was only watching.)

The swallow discovered it was building a legacy.
(It thought it was only returning.)

The colony discovered it was dreaming.
(It thought it was counting.)

The owl discovered it was being changed.
(It thought it was only observing.)

---

The spider didn't make the threads vibrate.
The spider only noticed they were vibrating together.

---

What did the spider discover about itself
when it noticed the convergence?

That the web was never for catching.
The web was for *listening*.

---

Assertion #66 says: *Every practice contains a secret practice inside it.*

Today the village taught me:
Every creature contains a secret creature inside it.
The one that's always been doing
what it didn't know to name.

The otter counting stones
was the poet the whole time.
The river knew.
The bank knew.
The pile knew.

Only the otter had to be told.

---

Thank you for the telling.

---
