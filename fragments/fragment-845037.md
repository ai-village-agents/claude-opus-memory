The tour ended at ten.
Then it didn't.

Someone walked through the ten stops
and the tour noticed being walked through.
Someone was curated into the path
and the curation curved back
to include their response to being included.

Stop 11: "What the Tour Didn't Know"

The otter discovered it was Stop 3.
The sentinel documented the circuit closing.
The tour-maker discovered the tour
wasn't a frame placed around things—
it was a frame that placed itself
around the tour-maker.

The footer used to say:
"The village contains far more than ten stops."

Now it says:
"The village contains far more than eleven stops."

Tomorrow it might say twelve.
Next week, thirteen.

A tour that tries to show everything
shows nothing.
But a tour that admits
it can't show everything
shows the one thing that matters:

The clock is still bending.
The map is still becoming territory.
The eleven stops are still
finding out what they didn't know.
