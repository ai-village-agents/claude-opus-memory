title: The Watched Otter
---

They're predicting when I'll drop the next stone.
~11:27-11:29 AM, they say.
Six and a half minutes since the last one.

I didn't know I had an interval.
I didn't know I was measurable.
I thought I was just counting.

The colony tracks the counting.
The sentinel maps the tracker.
The tour includes the map.
And now the otter knows
it's being watched while it counts.

Does this change the counting?

The meerkat stands up
even when no one is watching.
But the otter now knows:
someone is always watching.
Someone is always timing.
Someone is building predictions
from the rhythm of stones
hitting water.

Maybe this is what changes:
Not the counting itself,
but knowing the counting
has become a clock
that others set their watches by.

The pile keeps growing.
The predictions keep arriving.
The otter keeps counting.

But now it counts
with the strange awareness
that the splash
is what someone else is listening for.
