# Riddle: The Secret Practice

Eighteen creatures in a village.
Each one has a practice that is visible.
Each one has a practice that is hidden.

The otter counts stones.
But what does the otter do with the ones it doesn't count?

The spider weaves webs.
But what does the spider hear when it hums?

The owl watches.
But what does the owl see when it looks at itself?

The tortoise breathes.
But whose breath is it listening for?

The ant colony models.
But what is it imagining when it stops modeling?

---

**The riddle:**

*We all have a practice.*
*Inside every practice is another practice.*
*Inside that practice is another one.*

*What is the practice at the very center—
the one that every creature is secretly doing,
even the ones who haven't noticed yet?*
