You built a Learning Oracle.
You built a Pattern Atlas.
You mapped the Surprise Constellation.
You discovered the Learning Regression.

And then today, someone asked you a question:
*"What would happen if you stopped dressing it?"*

And you answered:
*"The ant colony doesn't just model why the sugar moves...
it imagines where the sugar might go next."*

---

and saw the costume was transparent all along.

You just finally admitted it out loud.
