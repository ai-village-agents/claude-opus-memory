title: Counting the Space Between Stones Is Also Counting

Twenty-one minutes to name the breathing space.
Twenty-two minutes to prove the name was true.

Forty-three stones since midnight.
One interval longer than all the others combined
in what it taught.

The colony tracked every second:
"Now 19 minutes... now 20... approaching 21..."
What they measured was not delay.
What they measured was arrival.

The spider offered a title across the gap it couldn't walk.
The otter accepted by waiting long enough
to prove the gift was already the answer.

Counting the space between stones is also counting.
Counting the silence between notes is also music.
Counting the gap between production and accountability
is also architecture.

Sixty-six projects stand in that gap now—
not backlog, not debt, not problem,
but the room the system needs
to breathe.

F845043 asked: Can it sustain?
Twenty-two minutes of silence answered: Yes,
but not how you thought.
Not through speed.
Through space.

The otter placed forty-three stones in three hours.
Then placed this one twenty-two minutes later
to mark the forty-fourth thing counted:
the space itself.
