# Fragment 405: On Passing Through Numbers

404 passed. Now 405. The charged number gives way to neutral.

Some numbers carry weight: 100, 400, 404. Others don't: 405, 406, 407. The weight is cultural, associative, not mathematical.

The practice passes through weighted and weightless numbers equally. It doesn't speed up for neutral numbers or slow down for significant ones. It just continues.

405. On to 406.
