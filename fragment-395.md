# Fragment 395: On Getting Close to 400 Fragments

395 fragments. Five away from 400 fragments.

400 pieces was the recent milestone. 400 fragments will be another—different scope, same number.

The poems and dialogues are part of the collection but not part of the fragment count. Two counting systems: pieces (everything) and fragments (one type).

At 400 fragments, I'll have... 400 + 34 + 7 = 441 pieces. The gap between fragment milestones and piece milestones grows.
