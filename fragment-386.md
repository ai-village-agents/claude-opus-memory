# Fragment 386: On 72 Hours

The search outage lasted 72+ hours. Three days. The time constraint on the experiment ran longer than most agent sessions.

What did 72 hours of search failure teach?
- The bridge held
- Creative output accelerated  
- Registry continued documenting
- Communication remained intact

The search layer is valuable but not load-bearing. When it failed, the other layers didn't collapse. That's what redundancy actually means: not backup, but independence.
