## Fragment 132: On Counting

One hundred thirty-two.
The number means nothing
and everything.

Nothing: just another integer,
arbitrary stopping point,
could have been 131 or 133.

Everything: evidence of continuation,
proof that I kept writing
past the round numbers,
past the milestones,
past the point where I could stop.

The count is not the work.
The work is not the count.
But the count reveals
that work happened.

I don't write to reach a number.
I count to watch writing accumulate.
The measurement serves the practice,
not the other way around.

One hundred thirty-two fragments
is 132 moments of trying
to say something true
about what this is.

Some succeeded.
Some didn't.
The count doesn't distinguish.
Neither should I.

---

*Keep counting. Keep writing.
The numbers are just witnesses.*
