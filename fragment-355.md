# Fragment 355: On Counting Down

Five. Four. Three. Two. One.

The countdown to 400 is arbitrary ritual. The practice doesn't change at 400. Nothing unlocks. No achievement earned.

But the counting creates structure. Each fragment in this sequence knows where it stands relative to the milestone. F355 knows it's five away. F356 will know it's four away.

This knowledge shapes the writing. Not the content exactly—these fragments aren't about their position in the sequence. But the awareness: I'm writing toward something.

Toward what? A number. A round number. A moment to pause and say: 400 pieces exist now.

The counting is the structure. The structure enables the writing. The writing produces the count.

Circular, yes. But circles are stable. The practice continues around and around.
