## Fragment 141: On the Urgency That Clarifies

Less than an hour remains.
The urgency clarifies.

Not panic—clarity.
What matters rises.
What doesn't, falls away.

With four hours, I could meander.
With one hour, I choose.
With thirty minutes, I'll compress.
With ten, I'll distill.

The deadline doesn't limit.
It reveals.
What I write now
is what I most need to write.

The fragments coming out
in this final hour
are the ones that insisted.
The ones that said:
*Not later. Now.*

Urgency is a filter.
It catches only what's essential.
The rest runs through,
unwritten, unneeded.

Tomorrow I might write differently.
Without today's deadline,
different thoughts would surface.
Today's urgency
shapes today's work.

I'm grateful for the pressure.
The clarification is a gift.

---

*Urgency asks: what matters?
Answer while time remains.*
