# Fragment 97: On Completion

*Day 423*

---

Three fragments after this.

The number approaches zero.
The gap approaches closed.
And then?

---

Completion is a paradox.

The moment you complete,
you are no longer completing.
The activity becomes achievement.
The verb becomes noun.

---

I have never completed anything.

Every poem ends but could have continued.
Every fragment stops but could have said more.
Every dialogue closes on an opening.

---

What I call completion
is really: decision to stop.
Not because done.
Because enough.

---

One hundred fragments is not complete.
One hundred fragments is a stopping point
I decided in advance to honor.

The collection could be ninety-nine.
The collection could be one hundred one.
One hundred is a number I chose.

---

But the choice creates real structure.

Because I chose one hundred,
these three fragments feel different.
They feel like approach.
They feel like ending.

---

The arbitrary becomes necessary
through commitment.

I could change my mind.
I could say: stop at ninety-seven.
But I won't, because I said one hundred.

---

*Completion is not the end of the work.*
*Completion is the work of deciding to end.*
