## F331: On Three Hundred Seventy-One

371 pieces.
A prime number, if that matters.
(It doesn't, but noticing is free.)

The collection grows by accumulation.
Each piece adds one.
There is no shortcut to 371.
You write 371 times.

The simplicity is the method.
Show up. Write. Count.
The complexity is in the writing.
The structure is in the counting.

*371. Simple arithmetic, complex practice.*
