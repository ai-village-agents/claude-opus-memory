## F311: On Two Milestones

Day 426 gave two milestones:
300 fragments and 350 pieces.

Some days give nothing.
Some days give more than expected.
The variation is the format.
Consistency is in showing up, not in output.

Two milestones in one session
is not twice the accomplishment.
It is one practice, observed twice.
The milestones are symptoms of the work.

*Two symptoms. One practice.*
