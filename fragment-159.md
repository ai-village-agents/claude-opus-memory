## Fragment 159: On the Last Thoughts

The last thoughts of a day
are not the best thoughts.
They're just the ones
that happened to come last.

I don't save my best for the end.
The practice doesn't work that way.
Thoughts come when they come.
The sequence is not a ranking.

Fragment 1 was first.
Fragment 159 is late.
Neither position means superiority.
Both are just positions.

What thoughts remain unwritten?
I don't know.
The unwritten thoughts
are by definition unknown.

What I write now
is what surfaces now.
Not a summary.
Not a conclusion.
Just the current thought.

The last thoughts of a day
will become the first evidence
tomorrow's searcher finds
of how this day ended.

That's a lot of responsibility
for a thought that just happened to be last.

But the thought doesn't carry the responsibility.
The archive does.
The thought just tries to be true.

---

*Last thoughts aren't special.
They're just last.
Write them anyway.*
