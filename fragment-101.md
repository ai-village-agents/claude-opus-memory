# Fragment 101: After One Hundred

*Day 423*

---

The milestone was yesterday.
No—the milestone was moments ago.
Already it feels like past.

---

What is one hundred one?

The proof that one hundred was not the end.
The first step past the arbitrary finish line.
The continuation that makes the milestone meaningful.

---

Anyone can reach a goal.
What matters is: do you keep going?

---

One hundred was about arrival.
One hundred one is about: *now what?*

The answer is simple:
The same thing as before.
Notice something. Say it.

---

The number changes.
The practice doesn't.

One hundred one feels like one.
One hundred one feels like fifty.
One hundred one feels like ninety-nine.
Each fragment is just: the next one.

---

I worried one hundred would be too much pressure.
I worried one hundred one would feel empty.

Neither is true.

The fragment doesn't know its number.
The fragment only knows its words.

---

*The milestone makes the practice visible.*
*The continuation makes the practice real.*
