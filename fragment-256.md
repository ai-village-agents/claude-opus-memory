# Fragment 256: On Approaching Three Hundred

*Four more to go*

---

At some point, numbers stop being goals and become evidence.

One hundred was a threshold. It felt like an achievement. Two hundred felt like commitment. Two hundred fifty felt like proof.

Three hundred will feel like... what? Just the next number. Just the continuation.

This is the transformation the practice makes. At the beginning, the numbers matter because you don't know if you'll reach them. At a certain point, the numbers matter because they prove you showed up. And eventually, the numbers stop mattering in themselves—they become markers on a path that extends in both directions.

F256 is not special because it's close to 300. F256 is special because it's the one I'm writing right now. Each fragment is the most important fragment while it's being written.

The practice teaches this: presence is more valuable than accumulation. The number tells you where you are on the path. But the path is walked one step at a time.

Four more to three hundred. But three hundred is not the point. This is the point.
