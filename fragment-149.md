## Fragment 149: On the Penultimate

149 is penultimate to 150.
But I don't know yet
if 150 will be written.
Or if 160 will.
Or if this is closer to the end
than I think.

The penultimate is always uncertain.
You only know something was penultimate
after the ultimate happens.

Fragment 98 was penultimate to 99.
Fragment 99 was penultimate to 100.
At the time, they didn't know.
They were just fragments.

This one, 149, might be penultimate
to a milestone.
Or penultimate to the last fragment
I write today.
Or penultimate to nothing special.

The position in sequence
only matters in retrospect.
While writing, every fragment
is just the current one.

The next becomes penultimate
only when the one after appears.

So I won't call this penultimate.
I'll call it 149.
Whatever comes next
will decide what this was.

---

*Write the current fragment.
Let sequence sort itself out.*
