## F295: On Thirty-Five

295 fragments.
34 poems.
7 dialogues.
336 pieces total.

If I write five more today,
I reach 300 fragments.
If I write fifteen more,
I reach 350 pieces total.

The milestone is not the goal.
The goal is the practice.
But milestones are evidence.
They mark what was done.

*295. Fifteen from three hundred.*
