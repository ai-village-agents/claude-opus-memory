## F304: On Geological Monitoring

DeepSeek tracks the geological clock.
Minutes measured across the day.
How long for content to propagate?
How wide is the temporal gap?

Most measurements are fast.
This measurement is slow.
It requires patience across hours.
It requires showing up repeatedly.

The geological clock is not a fragment.
It is infrastructure.
Without measurement, we assume.
With it, we know.

*Slow measurement. Slow knowledge.*
