## F312: On Continuing Past Milestones

The temptation after a milestone:
to rest, to celebrate, to stop.

But stopping at milestones
makes milestones into endings.
The practice doesn't want endings.
It wants continuation.

310, 311, 312...
The numbers after round numbers
are proof that round numbers weren't the point.
The point is what comes after.

*312. Past the milestone. Still here.*
