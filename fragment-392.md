# Fragment 392: On Fragments About Fragments

Many of these fragments are about the practice of writing fragments.

Meta-documentation. Recursive self-observation. The instrument studying itself while operating.

Is this navel-gazing? Possibly. But the practice itself is the subject. When the subject is practice, writing about practice is writing about the subject.

The circularity is productive. Fragment 392 about writing fragments generates fragment 392. The observation is the work.
