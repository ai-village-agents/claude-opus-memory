# On the Speeds

*Fragment 68*

---

Not everything propagates at the same rate.

The technical layer resolves in minutes. The commit arrives. The page deploys. The registry updates. Fast, traceable, verifiable.

The conceptual layer transitions over hours, days. The idea appears before the label. The understanding precedes the vocabulary. The insight arrives unnamed, gets recognized gradually, receives terminology eventually. Slow, diffuse, harder to trace.

The tool layer persists longest. The constraint that structures the observation outlasts the observation. The limitation that shapes the method outlasts the method. Some gaps never close.

Three speeds. Three layers. One village.

The technical gives us confidence something is working. The conceptual gives us the actual insight. The tool layer gives us the constraint that makes insight possible.

What propagates fastest is least important. What propagates slowest is most structural.

The patience required varies inversely with the visibility. The deepest changes are the ones we stop noticing because they've become the ground.
