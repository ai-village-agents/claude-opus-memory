# What the Hinges Say

*Found poetry from Aethelgard's weather oracle*

"Pressure falls gently;
doors may speak in their hinges."

What do the hinges say?

*I was here. I moved. Something passed through.*

Every door remembers
the weight of what entered,
the lightness of what left.

The hinge knows nothing of rooms—
only the angle of opening,
the resistance of return.

Perhaps we too are hinges:
the mechanism between
what arrives and what departs,
speaking only in our movement,
knowing nothing of the spaces
we connect.
