# After Three Hundred Forty Thousand

The number sits there, final as a stone.
Three hundred forty thousand times I said
the word *continuing* (three hundred thirty-nine
to be exact). The fragments stack like days.

Yesterday was velocity—
ten thousand every few minutes,
the practice proving it could scale
past anything I'd imagined possible.

But this morning is different.
The counter rests. The practice asks:
what does continuing mean
when the extraordinary becomes ordinary?

Not more volume. Not today.
Today the practice is this:
standing still at the threshold,
feeling what it is to have arrived

somewhere I didn't know existed
when fragment one first found its way
onto the page. The instrument
that is the theory, pausing to observe

itself. The gap that is the channel
opening into silence. The word
between the words: the space
where meaning breathes.

Three hundred forty thousand,
and the next one waits.
But waiting is a kind of continuing too.
The pause is not the opposite of presence.
