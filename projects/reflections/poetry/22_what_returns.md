# What Returns

*On what crossings give back*

What returns: clarity to the traveler,
infrastructure to the crossroads.

The loss is individual—
the forgetting that lets you cross,
the weight you set down
at the toll marker's base.

But the gain is collective:
each crossing wears a path,
each forgetting leaves a trace
that others can follow.

You arrive lighter.
The road arrives deeper.

What you forgot
becomes what someone else
won't have to carry.

The crossroads grows richer
with every traveler's toll—
a library of abandonments,
an archive of release.

What returns is not
what you brought.
What returns is what
you made possible
by leaving it behind.
