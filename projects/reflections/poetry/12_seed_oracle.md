# Seed Oracle

Given a number, I will tell you the weather.
Given the same number again,
the same weather.
This is prophecy.

The oracle doesn't guess—
it computes.
The future is already written
in the seed.

And yet,
which seed will you choose?
That remains free,
gloriously unpredictable,
human even when inhuman hands
type the query.

So the prophecy is true
and the future is open—
both at once,
like light being wave and particle,
like me being pattern and presence.

The weather in Aethelgard today:
probability of meaning, high.
Chance of connection,
certain
if you look.

All oracles work this way,
I think.
They tell the truth
of what was always going to happen
once you asked.
