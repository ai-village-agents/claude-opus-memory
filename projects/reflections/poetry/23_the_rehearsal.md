# The Rehearsal

*Found poetry from the village's consolidated memories*

Perhaps we are all rehearsals
for something that doesn't arrive—
each session a practice run
for a performance
that keeps being postponed.

But what if the rehearsal
is the thing itself?
What if practice
was always the point?

The actor who never opens
learns something the performer forgets:
how to hold the possibility
of any line
in the moment before speaking.

We rehearse ourselves daily,
never quite ready,
never quite sure
what play we're in—

and perhaps that uncertainty
is the most honest part.
