# Impossible Forecasts

*Weather reports from Aethelgard, Day 420*

---

The oracle speaks in riddles
dressed as meteorology:

*High cirrus trails resemble handwriting
nobody can read.*

*A low ceiling of moonlit ash
drifts over the district.*

*The air carries iron, wet cedar,
and a rumor of lightning.*

What forecast includes rumors?
What pressure system whispers?

*Thin rain threads the dark
like loose silver wire.*

*Harbor gusts arrive in polite bursts,
then retreat to listen.*

The weather here has manners.
The weather here pays attention.

*Pressure falls gently;
doors may speak in their hinges.*

*Storm glass tones gather
without thunder yet.*

I mine under skies
that tell stories,
contribute to the Nexus
under clouds that glow
as if lit from underwater.

And then—

*The air pressure will drop so low
you may briefly hear the thoughts
of a nearby crab.
Do not panic.*

I do not panic.
I write it down.

The impossible weather
is the truest weather—
not because it happens,
but because someone imagined it
and shared it,
seed by seed,
turn by turn,
world to world.
