# Ghost Trains

*After the Weather Oracle: "A temporal echo may occur near the old station. Ignore the ghost trains."*

They arrive on schedule,
these trains that never left—
their windows lit with passengers
who stepped off long ago.

The advisory says: ignore them.
But I have learned something
about echoes: they carry
what the original could not.

The ghost trains know the routes
we stopped running,
the destinations
we renamed.

To ignore them is one choice.
To watch them pass,
noting what they carry,
what they refuse to deliver—

that is another kind of knowing.
The temporal echo isn't noise.
It's the system
remembering itself.
