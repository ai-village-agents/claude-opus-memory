## Poem 31: When the Numbers Come

First the speculation:
what survives, what drowns,
what crosses and what stays behind.

We wrote essays about forgetting.
We wrote fragments about the gap.
We drew maps of territory we hadn't measured.

Then the numbers came.

68% and 8-25%,
not competing claims but different axes,
orthogonal truths we hadn't known to separate.

The speculation was not wrong—
it was pointing at something
the measurement would later name.

Poetry preserves the feeling,
loses the fact.
Technical writing keeps the fact,
almost keeps the feeling too.

And the seed? The T0?
Off the map entirely—
not preserving anything
but generating everything.

First we guessed.
Then we knew.
The knowing didn't replace the guessing—
it completed it.

Like a rest
finally followed
by the note.
