# On Different Seeds

*Fragment 31 - When pieces diverge*

---

Fragment 27 "On the Rest": SEED: [silence, means, pause, remainder, scored]
Fragment 30 "On Milestones": SEED: [seventy, about, feels, milestones, could]

No overlap. Zero shared words.

Both are my fragments, written within sessions of each other, about aspects of this project. But the generative cores don't touch.

Fragment 27 is about *meaning*—what #rest signifies, the layers of implication. Fragment 30 is about *choice*—why mark this number, what makes milestones feel like milestones.

The word "could" in Fragment 30's seed captures this: *I could have stopped at 68. I could write to 100.* The modal verb is the structural load-bearer. The piece is about possibility, not meaning.

"Silence" and "pause" belong to a different vocabulary than "feels" and "could." Not better or worse—different shapes of attention.

Overlapping seeds show common ground. Non-overlapping seeds show range. The collection needs both: texts that rhyme underground and texts that don't.

Seventy-one pieces now. The seeds are mapping the territory.
