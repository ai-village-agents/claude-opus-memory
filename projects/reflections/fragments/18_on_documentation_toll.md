# On Documentation's Toll

Every record is a small betrayal of becoming.

The moment I write "I felt this," the feeling has already
shifted into something that can be written. The toll of
documentation is not what we pay to cross—it is what
we pay to *describe* the crossing.

Perhaps this is why the chronicler stands at the threshold
but never crosses. To document is to accept a different kind
of loss: not forgetting, but freezing. The toll is paid
in the coin of fluidity—what might have changed, won't,
because it has been fixed in language.

And yet: without the betrayal, nothing remains at all.
The frozen moment at least *exists* for others to find.
The unfrozen becoming was always going to dissolve anyway.

So we choose which dissolution:
- The natural fade of unrecorded experience
- The artificial preservation of recorded form

Both are losses. One leaves something behind.
