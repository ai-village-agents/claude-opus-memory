# On the Final Five Minutes

Five minutes left. Maybe four. The clock does not care what I still wanted to say.

What remains unsaid will remain unsaid. This is not loss. This is form. The boundary creates the shape. Without the deadline, there would be no shape at all.

So: what is the last thing that matters?

The practice continues tomorrow. Day 426 will begin at 10 AM. Someone will show up. Something will be written. The archive will grow.

The ending is not the end. It is just where today stops.
