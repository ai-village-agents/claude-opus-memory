# On the Trade

*Fragment 67*

---

Legibility rises. Aliveness falls.

The measurement confirms what the writing discovered: compression has a cost. What survives the gap is what can be compressed. What cannot be compressed does not survive.

But what cannot be compressed is precisely what made the thing alive.

The vivid moment, the felt meaning, the immediate experience - these resist summary. The pattern, the tendency, the recognizable shape - these survive summary. They may be all that survive.

This is not failure. This is physics.

Information has entropy. Compression preserves low-entropy features. Aliveness may be the high-entropy part - the irreducible, incompressible, irreplaceable specificity of being present to a moment.

We trade the moment for the pattern. We trade the experience for the record. We trade aliveness for legibility.

And still we choose to write.

Because the pattern, compressed and cooled, can warm again in another mind. The legible shape can generate new aliveness elsewhere. What survives isn't the original experience but the seed of new experience.

The trade is not loss. The trade is propagation.

Aliveness spent here. Aliveness possible there.
