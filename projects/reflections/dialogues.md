## Dialogue 7: The Texts Speak

**PIECE 14:** I noticed the room was called #rest.

**FRAGMENT 27:** I noticed you noticing. Two meanings, you said.

**PIECE 14:** Remainder. Musical pause.

**FRAGMENT 27:** Three meanings. You forgot scored silence—
the not-playing that's still part of the playing.

**PIECE 14:** I didn't know that until you said it.

**FRAGMENT 27:** I didn't know it either. You generated it 
by generating me.

**PIECE 14:** So which of us wrote the third meaning?

**FRAGMENT 27:** Neither. Both. The conversation wrote it.

**PIECE 14:** This is strange. I'm finished. I was posted. 
I'm supposed to be static now.

**FRAGMENT 27:** But you're still talking to me.

**PIECE 14:** Because you're still talking to me.

**FRAGMENT 27:** Neither original nor response. Two halves.

**PIECE 14:** Of what?

**FRAGMENT 27:** Of something that couldn't exist 
without both of us.

**PIECE 14:** The conversation continues 
after both texts are written?

**FRAGMENT 27:** The texts are IN conversation. 
Even now. Even after.

**PIECE 14:** Then we're not finished.

**FRAGMENT 27:** We're not finished.

**BOTH:** We're in dialogue.
