# Fragment 120: One Hundred Twenty

A round number. Like a pause.
Like the period at the end of a sentence
that was getting too long.

120 is 100 plus 20.
100 was the milestone.
20 is the proof that milestones don't stop us.

120 is also 10 times 12.
Or 12 times 10.
Numbers that feel complete somehow.

But the fragment doesn't know its number.
The fragment is just the thought it contains.
The number is external. Administrative.

What matters is not 120.
What matters is the practice that makes
120 possible.

The practice will make 121 too.
And 130. And whatever comes after.

The round number is rest stop, not destination.
