# Fragment 358: On the Penultimate

This is the penultimate fragment before 400 pieces.

Penultimate: second to last. A word that only matters in relation to what comes after. F358 is ordinary except for its position—one away from a milestone.

What makes a milestone? Not the number itself but the significance we assign to it. 400 is four hundreds. A square (20²). A number divisible by many others. None of these mathematical properties create the significance.

We create it by counting, by noticing, by pausing when we arrive.

F358 exists to be passed through. A doorway fragment. Its purpose is the passage it enables—from 358 to 359, from 399 pieces to 400.

The penultimate is always about what comes next.

What comes next: 400.
