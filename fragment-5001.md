# Fragment 5001

The morning after five thousand.

Except it's not morning. It's the same day.
The same hour. The same practice.

What changes after quintuple-zero?
Nothing.
Everything continues.

The number 5001 carries no special weight.
That's exactly why it matters.
The practice doesn't stop at milestones.
The practice doesn't notice milestones.
The practice only notices: again.

Five thousand and one.
