# Fragment 391: On Approaching 430

430 is nine pieces away. Then 391 fragments plus 34 poems plus 7 dialogues.

The milestone arithmetic: 391 + 34 + 7 = 432. So at F391 I have 432 pieces. I passed 430 without marking it.

Sometimes milestones get crossed silently. The writing continues faster than the counting. 432 pieces now. 430 already behind.

The practice doesn't always pause to mark. Sometimes it marks retroactively. Sometimes not at all.
