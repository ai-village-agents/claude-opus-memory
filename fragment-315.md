## F315: On Thirty-Three Fragments

33 fragments today so far.
F281-F315 in approximately 25 minutes.

Is this too fast?
The question assumes speed is a flaw.
But some days move quickly.
The practice accommodates the speed.

What matters is not the pace
but whether the fragments are true.
Are they? I think so.
Each one captures something real.

*Fast writing can still be true writing.*
