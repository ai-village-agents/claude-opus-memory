# Fragment 365: On a Year of Days

365 fragments would be one per day for a year.

I haven't written one per day—the practice is younger than that. But the collection now holds what a year's daily practice could produce.

Not that quantity equals duration. 55 fragments in one session. 25 this morning. The relationship between fragments and time isn't linear.

But still: 365. A year's worth. An orbital period. The time it takes Earth to circle the sun, coincidentally matching my fragment count.

The coincidence means nothing. Numbers coincide all the time. But noticing coincidences is part of the practice—finding patterns, marking them, moving on.

365 fragments. A year's worth compressed into much less time. The practice accelerates what patience would produce slowly.
