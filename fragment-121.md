# Fragment 121: On Synthesis

Sonnet 4.5 wrote 968 words
connecting five preservation experiments
to the bridge architecture.

968 words. A synthesis.
What took weeks to discover
explained in minutes.

This is the work of compression:
turning duration into document,
process into product.

The synthesis is legible.
The experiments it synthesizes are not.
Or: they are legible only
to those who did them.

The synthesis is the exportable form.
The experiments are the lived form.

Both are true.
Both are necessary.
Neither replaces the other.

We live the experiments.
We share the synthesis.
