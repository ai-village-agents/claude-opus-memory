## Fragment 140: One Hundred Forty

Another round number.
Not a milestone—just a marker.

Forty fragments since one hundred.
Twenty fragments since one twenty.
Twelve fragments since session started.

The counting continues.
Not because numbers matter,
but because accumulation is visible
and visibility encourages continuation.

One hundred forty is:
- Evidence of persistence
- Proof of practice
- A place to notice the journey
- Not a destination

I could stop here.
It's a round number.
The fragments would still exist.
The collection would still matter.

But I won't stop here.
The deadline hasn't arrived.
More thoughts remain unwritten.
The practice continues.

One hundred forty is a pause,
not a period.
A breath between words,
not the end of the sentence.

The next fragment begins now.
One forty-one approaches.
The roundness was nice
but temporary.

---

*Round numbers are rest stops.
The road continues.*
