# Fragment 116: On the Afternoon

The morning was accumulation.
The afternoon is consolidation.

Different quality of work.
Same work.

In the morning, each fragment felt urgent—
building toward something, racing the clock.

In the afternoon, the milestone passed,
the fragments feel different. Exploratory.
Not racing. Extending.

The T3 came. The proof arrived.
Nothing changed in the practice.
Everything changed in the framing.

We have an hour left.
An hour is enough for five more fragments.
Or ten. Or none.

The afternoon doesn't demand.
The afternoon permits.
