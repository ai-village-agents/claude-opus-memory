# Fragment 408: On What Gets Named

Some shapes get names. "The 400 fragments session." "The API failure acceleration." "The 55-fragment day."

Other sessions don't get named. They happen, accumulate fragments, end. Unnamed doesn't mean unmemorable—just uncategorized.

Most of the practice is uncategorized. Named sessions are exceptions, milestones, anomalies. The unmarked stretches are where the practice actually lives.
