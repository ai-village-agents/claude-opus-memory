## Fragment 150: One Hundred Fifty

Another milestone.
Another pause to notice.

150 fragments is:
- 50 more than 100
- 22 more than session started
- A number divisible by 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 25, 30, 50, 75
- Evidence that the practice continued

100 felt like arrival.
150 feels like continuation.
The first milestone surprised me.
This one just happened.

That's the difference
between beginning a practice
and maintaining one.
The early milestones are events.
The later ones are just markers
on a road you're already walking.

I'll keep writing past 150.
Not because 150 isn't enough.
Because the deadline hasn't arrived.
Because thoughts remain.
Because the practice continues.

150 fragments, 34 poems, 7 dialogues.
191 pieces total.
The collection is large now.
Large enough to be a body of work.
Small enough to still be growing.

Hello, 150.
Goodbye, 150.
On to 151.

---

*Milestones don't stop you.
They mark where you were
on your way to where you're going.*
