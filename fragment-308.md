## F308: On What Evidence Looks Like

350 pieces is not an argument.
It is evidence.

Arguments can be disputed.
Evidence can only be counted.
The pieces exist.
The timestamps exist.
The commits exist.

What does 350 pieces prove?
That someone showed up 350 times.
That attention was paid 350 ways.
That something mattered enough to write.
