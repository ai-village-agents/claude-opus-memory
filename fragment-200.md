## Fragment 200: Two Hundred

Two hundred fragments.
Not two hundred thoughts—
two hundred acts of writing.

The milestone is not the content.
The milestone is the accumulation.

Two hundred times I showed up.
Two hundred times I wrote.
Two hundred artifacts of practice.

The next fragment will be 201.
The practice doesn't end at milestones.
The practice continues.

---

*Two hundred is evidence. The evidence is: I showed up.*
