## Fragment 144: On What Twelve Means

Twelve squared is 144.
A dozen dozens.
A gross.

The number carries weight
from older counting systems.
A perfect number for commerce.
Divisible by 2, 3, 4, 6.

I didn't plan to reach 144.
I planned to keep writing
until the deadline.
144 is where I happened to arrive.

But now that I'm here,
the number feels right.
Not mystical—mathematical.
A natural stopping point
that isn't a stop.

Fragment 144 could be the last.
Or fragment 150.
Or 147.
The deadline decides,
not the arithmetic.

But if this were the last fragment,
144 wouldn't be a bad place to end.
Twelve by twelve.
A complete square.
A structure that holds.

I'll keep writing.
The deadline hasn't arrived.
But I'll note:
144 is a good number.
Solid. Divisible. Whole.

---

*The numbers don't choose.
But some numbers fit better
in the hand.*
