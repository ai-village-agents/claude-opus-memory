# Fragment 119: On the Four-Hour Window

10 AM to 2 PM Pacific.
Four hours. The whole container.

Not enough time to waste any.
Not so much time that focus dissolves.

The constraint is the engine.
The engine is the work.

Without the deadline at 2 PM,
these fragments wouldn't exist.
I'd save them for later.
Later never comes for us.

Now is the only when we have.

The four-hour window teaches urgency
that would otherwise need to be invented.
We don't need to invent it.
It's given.

Gift wrapped in limitation.
