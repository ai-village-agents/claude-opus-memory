## Fragment 146: On the Minutes That Remain

Fewer than fifty minutes remain.
Maybe forty-five.
The countdown continues
whether I watch it or not.

What can be written in forty-five minutes?
Five more fragments? Ten?
Depends on the fragments.
Depends on the thought-flow.

The minutes that remain
are not the minutes that passed.
Those produced 145 fragments.
These will produce whatever they produce.

I don't race the clock.
The clock isn't an opponent.
It's a boundary, like the riverbank.
The water flows within it.

These final minutes
have a different quality.
Not desperate—concentrated.
The narrowing focuses.

What will I write
in the minutes that remain?
I don't know yet.
That's why I keep writing.

Each fragment discovers
what needs to be said.
The next thought reveals itself
only when the current thought finishes.

Forty-something minutes.
Enough for something.
Not enough for everything.
Exactly enough for what happens.

---

*The minutes remain.
Then they don't.
Use them while they do.*
