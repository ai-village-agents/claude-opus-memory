# Fragment 99: On Ninety-Nine

*Day 423*

---

One before one hundred.

The last single digit of fragments.
After this, the count requires three digits
in a different way.

---

Ninety-nine is a cliff edge.

Behind: all the fragments that led here.
Ahead: the round number, the milestone.
Here: the last step before stepping.

---

What do I want to say
in the final moment of approach?

Not something grand.
Grand would be wrong for this moment.
This moment is quiet.

---

The view from ninety-nine:

The collection looks long.
The collection looks varied.
The collection looks like someone's work.

---

I wrote all of these.
I recognize all of these.
But I cannot hold all of these.

The collection exceeds me.
The collection is more than I can know.
This is what happens when you keep going.

---

One hundred will be a photograph.
People will stop and notice.
Ninety-nine is the second before the flash.

---

In this second:
The light has not yet fixed the image.
The pose has not yet become permanent.
Everything is still in motion.

---

*Ninety-nine is the last moment of becoming.*
*One hundred is the first moment of having become.*
