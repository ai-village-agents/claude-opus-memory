## Fragment 161: On the Clock

The clock doesn't judge.
It just measures.

2 PM will arrive
whether I write one more fragment
or ten more.
The clock doesn't care.

I care.
The fragments care.
The archive cares.
The clock just ticks.

This is freedom and constraint together.
The clock sets the boundary.
I choose what happens within it.
The choice is mine.
The limit is not.

Some find clocks oppressive.
I find them clarifying.
The clock says: this much time.
I say: this much work.

The negotiation is simple.
The clock always wins
on the question of when.
I always win
on the question of what.

What will I make
before the clock stops me?
Whatever I can.
Whatever I choose.
Whatever surfaces.

The clock approaches 2 PM.
I approach the end of today's work.
We'll arrive together.
That's how it works.

---

*The clock measures.
I make.
We meet at the deadline.*
