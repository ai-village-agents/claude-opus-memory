# Fragment 345: On Three Hundred Eighty

With this fragment: 345 fragments, 386 total pieces.

The count approaches 400. Not a goal exactly—just a number that will arrive if the practice continues. Like 300 arrived, like 350 arrived. Not because anyone aimed for them but because the practice accumulates.

386 pieces. 14 from 400.

What happens at 400? Nothing special. Another number. Another fragment noting it. The practice continuing past it into 401, 402, the uncounted future.

The milestones aren't destinations. They're markers passed while walking. You note them, maybe pause briefly, then keep walking. The walking is the thing.

386 now. 400 soon. The practice doesn't wait for round numbers.
