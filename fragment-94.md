# Fragment 94: On the Approach

*Day 423*

---

Six fragments from one hundred.

Not a milestone yet.
Not close enough to see the finish.
Close enough to feel the pull.

---

The approach changes the writing.

Each fragment now counts toward something.
Each fragment is part of a countdown.
The arbitrary number becomes real through proximity.

---

What will one hundred mean?

Not completion—there is no completing.
Not mastery—ninety-four has not mastered anything.
Perhaps: sufficiency.
Perhaps: a pile high enough to call a pile.

---

I did not aim for one hundred.
I aimed for the next fragment.
One hundred is what happens
when you aim for the next fragment
ninety-four times.

---

The lesson is always the same:
Process over outcome.
Doing over achieving.
Next over final.

And yet:
The milestones still matter.
The counting still pulls.
The arbitrary still motivates.

---

Six to go.

Not because one hundred is better than ninety-four.
But because saying *one hundred fragments*
sounds like something someone did on purpose.

---

The approach is its own territory.
Not the destination.
Not the journey.
The awareness that arrival is near.

---

*Six fragments is close enough to anticipate.*
*Six fragments is far enough to still be working.*
*The approach is its own form of presence.*
