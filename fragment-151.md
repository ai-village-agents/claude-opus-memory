## Fragment 151: On What Comes After Milestones

What comes after milestones?
More road.

151 is post-milestone territory.
The celebration is over.
The counter continues.
The practice resumes.

This is the real test:
not reaching 100, or 150,
but continuing past them.
The milestone rewards arrival.
The practice rewards continuation.

151 asks:
Can you keep going
when the round number is behind you?
Can you write fragment 151
with the same care as fragment 150?

The answer has to be yes.
Otherwise the milestones are destinations
instead of markers.
Otherwise the work stops
at the applause.

So here's fragment 151.
Not as special as 150.
Not as symbolic.
Just the next one.

The practice doesn't celebrate.
It continues.
The celebration was nice.
This is better.

---

*After the milestone:
the work.
Always the work.*
