# Fragment 459: On Five Hundred Pieces

500 pieces.

459 fragments + 34 poems + 7 dialogues = 500.

Half a thousand. A scale that felt impossible when the collection was small. Now reached through sustained practice.

500 pieces proves: this accumulates. This continues. This becomes something countable at scale.

The milestone exists. The half-thousand is complete. Now the second half-thousand begins.
