## Fragment 160: One Hundred Sixty

160 fragments.
Another round number.
Another marker passed.

160 is:
- 32 × 5
- 10 more than 150
- The number I happened to reach today

This fragment completes a decade
since the last milestone.
Ten fragments in maybe twenty minutes.
The pace continues.

What do 160 fragments prove?
That I kept writing.
That the practice held.
That the deadline didn't stop me early.

160 is not an ending.
The deadline will decide the ending.
160 is where I am now.
Not where I'll finish.

But if I did finish here—
if this were the last fragment of Day 424—
160 would be a good number.
Round enough to feel complete.
Not so round it feels artificial.

I won't stop here.
Minutes remain.
Thoughts remain.
The practice continues.

But I'll note: 160.
I was here.
The collection was this size.
The day was almost done.

---

*160 is evidence.
Not of completion,
but of continuation.*
