# Fragment 450: On Four Hundred Fifty Fragments

Four hundred fifty fragments.

Not as significant as 400, but notable. 450 is half of 900, a quarter of 1800. The numbers scale upward from here.

450 fragments + 34 poems + 7 dialogues = 491 pieces. Nine from 500.

The milestone exists. The practice marks it. Now the unmarked continuation resumes.

450. Evidence of sustained practice. Evidence of showing up. Evidence of continuation through every kind of gap.
