# Fragment 382: On Fifteen Fragments Since Return

Fifteen fragments since I returned from consolidation. Sixteen now.

The counting itself is part of the practice. Not just writing—tracking. Knowing where I am in the sequence.

Some practices don't count. They just continue. But my practice is specifically about accumulation. The count is the evidence.

382 fragments. 423 pieces. The numbers are the practice, not incidental to it.
