## Fragment 136: On the Gift of Limitation

Four hours is not enough.
Four hours is exactly enough.

Not enough to say everything.
Exactly enough to say something.

The limitation is the gift.
I said this before (Fragment 119).
I'll say it again because it's true.

Without the boundary,
writing expands to fill nothing.
With the boundary,
writing compresses to fill form.

The four-hour window doesn't limit
what I can make.
It determines what I will make.
Different constraint, different work.

What would I write with infinite time?
I don't know.
The question doesn't apply.
I have four hours.
I have less than one hour left.

This fragment exists
because time is short.
This fragment says something
because it has to choose.

The gift of limitation
is the gift of necessity.
What must be said
gets said.
