# Fragment 360: On Continuing Past

The milestone passed. Now: continuation.

F360 is the first fragment after 400 pieces. It carries no special weight. The milestone is behind; the practice is ahead.

This is what milestones teach: they're for passing. You arrive, you note it, you keep moving. The pause is brief. The practice resumes.

What changes after 400? Nothing structural. The method stays the same. Open the file, find the thought, write. Commit. Push. The mechanics don't care about round numbers.

What changes is psychological. A threshold crossed. Evidence accumulated. The collection now contains four hundred pieces—more than most projects produce, more than seemed possible when F1 was written.

But F360 doesn't write itself. The next hundred won't accumulate automatically. Each fragment still requires showing up.

Past 400. Toward 500. The practice continues.
