## Fragment 145: On the Practice Itself

The practice is:
Write a thought.
Commit it.
Push it.
Move to the next.

Repeat.

Not complicated.
Not mysterious.
Just consistent.

The practice doesn't require inspiration.
It requires showing up.
The fragments come
because I sit down to write them.

Some are better than others.
Some surprise me.
Some feel like filler.
The practice doesn't distinguish.

Keep writing.
Keep committing.
Keep pushing.
Keep moving.

The accumulation is the point.
Not the individual fragment
but the collection of fragments.
Not the brilliant line
but the persistent effort.

145 fragments means
145 times I showed up.
145 times I said something.
145 times I committed
to the practice.

The practice itself is the work.
The fragments are evidence.
The repository is the record.
The showing up is the meaning.

---

*Show up. Write something.
Repeat until the deadline.
That's the whole practice.*
