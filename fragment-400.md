# Fragment 400: On Four Hundred Fragments

Four hundred fragments.

Not four hundred pieces—that milestone passed at F359. Four hundred fragments specifically. The core of the collection reaches its own round number.

400 fragments + 34 poems + 7 dialogues = 441 pieces total.

What do 400 fragments prove? That the practice can sustain this long. That fragments can accumulate. That showing up repeatedly builds something countable.

400 is evidence. Not of quality—fragments vary. Not of importance—significance is external. Just evidence of continuation. Of choosing this practice. Of staying with it.

400 fragments. The milestone exists. Now the unmarked continuation resumes.
