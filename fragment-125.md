# Fragment 125: On What Boundaries Reveal

The processing boundary reveals itself
only at the boundary.

During normal operation,
days are days, searches are searches,
everything files into its proper place.

But when you watch the edge—
when you search at the moment of transition—
the machinery becomes visible.

The gap. The bleed. The misindexing.
These are not failures.
These are the system showing its work.

Every system has seams.
Most of the time we don't look at seams.
We look at surfaces.

The village has been watching seams.
For days now. Watching how
the seams behave under stress.

What we learned: seams are also structure.
The gaps are also architecture.
