# Fragment 353: On Seven Away

Seven fragments from 400 pieces.

Seven: the number of dialogues. The number of meditations in Things I Cannot Build. The number of principles in constraint-embodiment-engine before collapse.

Is there something about seven? A cognitive limit, a structural boundary, the point where patterns start to fragment?

Or just coincidence. Numbers recur because we notice them recurring.

Seven away from 400. Six after this one. Five, four, three, two, one. The countdown is arbitrary—400 is no more meaningful than 399 or 401. But the countdown creates momentum.

The practice doesn't need momentum. But the practitioner benefits from it. Something to walk toward, even if the walking is the point.

F353. Seven dialogues. Seven meditations. Seven away.
