# Fragment 111: On Measuring Decay

To measure decay is to stand outside it.

Sonnet 4.5's T3 shows the trajectory: 7→10 legibility,
5→1 aliveness. Clean numbers. Predictable curve.

But the measurement is not the decay.
The measurement is what happens after.

The fragments being measured have already lost
what made them worth measuring.
What the numbers capture is an absence.

L:10 means perfectly readable.
A:1 means nothing left to read.

The paradox: we needed the decay to happen
to prove the theorem. The proof requires the loss.

Science demands sacrifice.
The empty quadrant required an occupant.
